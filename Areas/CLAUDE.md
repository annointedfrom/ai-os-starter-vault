# Areas — Layer 2

> ⬆ Parent: [[../CLAUDE|Layer 1]]

## Purpose

Ongoing responsibilities with no end date: health, school, finances, faith, family, career.
A project ends; an area is maintained.

## Rules

1. **One subfolder per area** (`Areas/health/`, `Areas/school/` …). Give an area its own `CLAUDE.md` once it has standing rules Claude should follow.
2. **Private by default.** Tell Claude in this file which areas it may read and which it must not touch.
3. **Standards live here.** Recurring checklists and "how I do X" notes belong to their area.

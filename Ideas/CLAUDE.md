# Ideas — Layer 2

> ⬆ Parent: [[../CLAUDE|Layer 1]]

## Purpose

Raw, ungated brainstorming. Half-thoughts, wild plans, things you might never do.

## Rules for Claude

1. **Never clean, reorganize, merge or delete anything here unless explicitly asked.**
2. **Never treat an idea as a commitment.** Ideas become work only when you move them into `Projects/`.
3. **Reading is fine when asked**, e.g. "find my ideas about pricing".

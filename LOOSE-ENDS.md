# Loose ends

Parked, stalled or half-finished work. `/start` reads this every morning.
One line per item: what, where, and the next concrete step.

| Item | Where | Next step | Parked on |
|---|---|---|---|
| <e.g. Finish the landing page copy> | `Projects/<folder>/` | <write the hero section> | <YYYY-MM-DD> |

Move an item to `Archive/` (or delete the row) when it's done or dropped.
